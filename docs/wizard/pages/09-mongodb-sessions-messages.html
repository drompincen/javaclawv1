9 MongoDB: sessions & messages

Ephemeral execution contexts + ordered conversation history

Execution contexts for agent conversations. Deleted on restart — transient computation state.

messageshigh volume
Chat history ordered by monotonic seq per session. Supports multimodal content (text + images).
messageIdsessionIdseqrolecontentparts[]agentIddurationMsmocked
Read: AgentLoop (context building), SessionController (UI retrieval)
Write: AgentLoop (save LLM responses), IntakePipeline (user intakes)
Compound unique: {sessionId, seq} — guarantees ordering
WHY ephemeral sessions? The meaningful data (threads, things, memories) lives in permanent collections. Sessions represent transient computation — stale sessions would cause confusion about what's "running".
WHY monotonic seq? LLMs are sensitive to message ordering. The seq field with a compound unique index guarantees deterministic replay regardless of network timing or concurrent writes.
