1 Business Value & Purpose

Continuous project intelligence — paste content, get structured insights

The Problem
Data fragmented across Jira, Docs, Confluence, Smartsheet. No single source of truth. Misalignments unnoticed. Status updates manual. Risk detection reactive.
The Solution
Multi-agent platform that ingests raw content, structures it into threads, tickets, objectives & plans, then continuously reconciles to detect drift, gaps, and risks.
Engineering Managers
Paste meeting notes → structured threads with action items. Ask "What's our sprint coverage?" — answers grounded in real data.
Project & Product Managers
Auto ticket extraction from Jira. Sprint objective synthesis with coverage %. Phase/milestone creation. Delta packs challenge inconsistencies.
Scrum Masters
ORR checklists auto-generated. Capacity analysis. Recurring reconciliation detects scope drift. 9am agents prep your standup.
Future: Tickets flagged "code-ready" dispatched to autonomous coding agents (Claude Code, Devin). Reviewer validates. JavaClaw orchestrates human intent to machine execution.
